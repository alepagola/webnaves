NES Line

Compact, Universal Monoblock Industrial Pump

General Description

The NES Line stands out for its universal-application monoblock configuration, where the drive couples directly to the pump. This compact design eliminates the need for long baseplates and extra bearings, resulting in a highly efficient solution for tight spaces.

It guarantees continuous, gentle transfer that's completely free of pulsation, delivering a flow rate directly proportional to rotational speed. It's the definitive equipment for pumping highly viscous media (up to 1,000,000 mPas), thixotropic and abrasive fluids, and even media with solids up to 150 mm.

Technical Specifications

Maximum Flow Rate Up to 400 m³/h (1,800 GPM)
Maximum Pressure Up to 24 bar (350 PSI)
Suction Capacity High, up to 9 m.w.c. (30 ft)
Operating Temperature -15°C to 300°C
Allowable Viscosity From 1 mPas up to 1,000,000 mPas
Max. Solids Passage Up to 150 mm
Coupling & Assembly Type Direct monoblock (horizontal or vertical position)
Sealing Systems Mechanical seal (single, double, cartridge) or PTFE packing

Thanks to its versatility, it's widely used across many industry segments:

  • Environment & Energy: Sludge, dewatered biosolids, and wastewater.
  • Chemical & Pulp/Paper: Adhesives, chemical pastes, toxic or aggressive fluids (pH 1-14).
  • Oil & Gas: Transfer of hydrocarbons, abrasive fluids, or lubricants.
  • Food & Pharmaceutical: Shear-sensitive products.

By integrating the motor and/or gearbox directly, both investment and maintenance costs are significantly reduced. It requires no field alignment, its assembly allows mounting in either horizontal or vertical position, and the flow direction can be easily reversed according to process needs. It also features an easy-to-service sealing system that doesn't require removing the entire drive.

Why choose the NES Line?
Total Versatility: Configurable rotor/stator geometries to prioritize either pressure or flow rate as your application requires.
Quiet Operation: Smooth, vibration-free, and highly energy-efficient performance.
Suction Power: Outstanding dry-vacuum suction capacity of up to 9 meters of water column.
