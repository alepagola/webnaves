Vertical NEA Line

High-Performance Industrial Submersible Design

Immersion / Vertical

General Description

The Vertical NEA Line is a robustly engineered unit designed for direct mounting on pits, tanks, or walls. Its pumping elements operate permanently submerged in the fluid, ensuring constant and efficient suction.

Its industrial-grade construction handles aggressive or neutral fluids of very high viscosity, with a high content of solid particles (up to 70 mm) or fibers. It includes a suction strainer and a template-drilled mounting flange for secure, turbulence-free anchoring.

Technical Specifications

Max Flow Rate Up to 128 m³/h (563 US GPM)
Differential Pressure Up to 24 bar (348 psi) | Available in 1, 2, or 4 stages
Operating Temperature Up to 140° C (284° F)
Allowable Viscosity 1 to 1,000,000 cP
Max. Solids Size Up to 70 mm (2.75 in)
Mounting Template-drilled support flange per ANSI B16.5 #150 FF
Coupling Configuration Monoblock with adapter lantern (hollow shaft and key) or bare shaft
Discharge Orientation 90° discharge casing with axial end suction (strainer)

  • Fluid transfer and reprocessing batteries.
  • Drain sump tank and API separator emptying.
  • Extraction from Knock-Out Drums (KOD) and condensate pumping.
  • Industrial effluents with very high solids or fiber content.
  • Truck loading/unloading operations and PIAS systems.

The drive unit (gear motor) couples directly and concentrically to the pump through an adapter lantern, using a standard solid shaft. This architecture ensures that all axial loads are absorbed by the pump's bearing, protecting the motor and delivering turbulence-free pumping that preserves the fluid's structure.

Why choose the Vertical NEA?
Safe Installation: Standard ANSI support flange and strainer suction for direct installation in the sump.
Integrated Support: The design absorbs axial loads, dramatically extending the service life of the drive system.
Continuous High Pressure: Available in modules of up to 4 stages, allowing pressure to be increased proportionally to RPM.
