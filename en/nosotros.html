About Us

Argentine Manufacturer of GECE PUMPS® Progressing Cavity Pumps

GECE PUMPS® is a registered trademark of Naves del Sur S.A., dedicated to manufacturing and marketing a complete line of eccentric screw (progressing cavity) pumps for every type of industry.

At our 1,800 m² industrial plant in Burzaco, Buenos Aires, we operate state-of-the-art CNC machinery and employ highly trained personnel. We manufacture progressing cavity pumps for every industry: oil and gas, chemical, wastewater, food and beverage, cement, paints, drilling muds, and dosing.

If you have a pumping challenge or need spare parts, do not hesitate to contact us.
