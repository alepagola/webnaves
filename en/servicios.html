Progressing Cavity Pump Repair & Spare Parts

Applied engineering to optimize your equipment's performance. Any brand.

More than a supplier: your technical partner in progressing cavity pumps

We don't just manufacture pumps; we offer engineering solutions to repair, adapt, and upgrade your existing pumping equipment, regardless of the brand (Netzsch®, Moyno®, Seepex®, PCM®, and more).

Progressing Cavity Pump Retrofitting

Technical independence and guaranteed savings on spare parts.

Do you own pumps from other brands (Netzsch®, Moyno®, Seepex®) where spare parts are expensive or take months to arrive?

We carry out the adaptation engineering required to modify your existing equipment, allowing it to run on our standard progressing cavity pump spare parts.

  • Drastic reduction in maintenance costs.
  • Spare parts delivered in 48/72 hours.
  • No modifications to the original piping or installation.

(We have no commercial affiliation with the brands mentioned above)

Custom Multi-Brand Spare Parts

We manufacture compatible parts for any progressing cavity pump brand on the market: Netzsch®, Moyno®, Seepex®, PCM®, and more. We rely on scanning technology and reverse engineering to reproduce rotors, stators, and shafts with millimeter precision.

We can also upgrade the original materials (e.g., switching from carbon steel to stainless steel, or changing the elastomer compound) to extend the service life of your pumping equipment.

Comprehensive Progressing Cavity Pump Repair

We have a specialized department for the repair of progressing cavity pumps, both our own and third-party equipment. The service includes:

  • Teardown and detailed technical diagnosis.
  • Shot blasting and epoxy painting.
  • Replacement of seals, bearings, and O-rings.
  • Hydraulic bench testing prior to delivery.

Commissioning & Technical Assistance

We offer on-site technical assistance for the start-up of new pumping equipment. We train your maintenance and operations staff on the proper use, torquing, and preventive care of progressing cavity pumps, ensuring trouble-free operation from day one.

Engineering & Technical Documentation

We provide the most complete technical documentation in the market to integrate our equipment into your projects:

  • 3D and 2D (CAD) drawings.
  • Data books (welding qualifications, procedures).
  • Test protocols and material certificates.

Nationwide Logistics & Delivery

We understand the urgency of your operation. We run our own fleet to deliver progressing cavity pumps and spare parts to:

  • Buenos Aires City and Greater Buenos Aires.
  • The rest of the country via freight carriers and express couriers.

We make sure equipment reaches its destination on time and in perfect condition, with packaging suited to protect it in transit.
