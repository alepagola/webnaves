J Line

Hopper & Auger Feed (J Line)

Forced feeding for low-flow products.

The definitive solution for media that won't flow by gravity. Its sloped-wall hopper and oversized feed auger force material into the stator, eliminating cavitation in dense sludge (filter cake), pastes, and heavy organic waste.
